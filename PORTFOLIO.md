# Portfolio entry

**SignalDesk AI — AI Technical Support Copilot**

Full-stack support engineering platform that classifies technical incidents, retrieves evidence from an internal knowledge base, produces cited troubleshooting workflows, and escalates low-confidence or high-severity cases for human review.

**Stack:** React, TypeScript, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite/PostgreSQL-ready, Docker, GitHub Actions

**Suggested portfolio bullets**
- Evidence-backed retrieval with visible knowledge citations instead of an opaque chatbot response.
- Severity, confidence, and escalation logic designed around human-in-the-loop support workflows.
- Dockerized API/frontend with persistence and automated tests.
